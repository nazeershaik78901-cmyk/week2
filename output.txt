EXPERIMENT 2: BASIC PROMPT ENGINEERING

USER PROMPT:
Explain prompt engineering in simple English with one example.

MODEL RESPONSE:
**Prompt engineering** is simply the art of asking an AI the right questions, in the right way, to get the best possible answer. 

Think of AI like a very smart assistant: if your instructions are vague, the result will be average. But if your instructions are clear and specific, the result will be great.

---

### Example: Asking for an email

* **A basic prompt:**  
  > *"Write an email asking for a day off."*  
  *(The AI will give you a generic, robot-sounding template that might not fit your situation.)*

* **An "engineered" prompt:**  
  > *"Write a short, polite email to my manager asking for this Friday off for a doctor's appointment. Keep it under 50 words and friendly in tone."*  
  *(The AI will give you an exact, ready-to-send email because you told it the **role**, the **reason**, the **length**, and the **tone**.)*

**In short:** Prompt engineering is just knowing how to give clear directions so the AI does exactly what you want on the first try.
